Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version: you can use Jama'ah Beacon without an account, and then we store nothing that identifies you. If you choose to sign in, we store your email, the name you choose, and which jama'ahs you host or join. Because these are prayer gatherings, that can reveal your religion, so we only do it with your explicit consent. You can delete your account at any time. We don't sell data or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

Jama'ah Beacon (jammah-beacon.vercel.app) is run by Amaan Omar, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, email amaan78940@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

  • Your location is used on your device to centre the map and show distances. It isn't sent to us, except for the pin you place when you host a beacon.
  • Beacons you host are public: the prayer, time, location pin, location label and directions, amenities, and any photo you add. Your device keeps a private key so it can edit or cancel the beacon. Photos are shrunk and their hidden metadata (including GPS position) is removed on your device before upload.
  • Joining ("Count me in") adds one to a public count. Nothing about who joined is shown.
  • Reports are stored with a random ID generated on your device, so one device counts once. It isn't linked to you.
  • Abuse prevention: to limit spam, we briefly store how many requests come from each IP address, in hourly or daily buckets, and delete them within 2 days.
  • Usage statistics are daily totals only (for example, "12 beacons created today"), with no IP addresses, device IDs or other identifiers.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. When you sign in, we store:

  • your email address, to sign you in;
  • if you use Google, the basic profile Google shares (your name, email and profile picture link), which our authentication provider keeps with your account;
  • the display name you choose, and whether to show it on beacons you host;
  • when you gave consent;
  • which beacons and regular spots you host, and which beacons you join.

Only your display name is ever shown to others, and only if you choose to show it. Who hosted or joined a beacon is never visible to other users.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Account data can reveal religious belief, which is special-category data under the UK GDPR. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give on the sign-in screen. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • Beacons are published because you choose to publish them, so that others can join.
  • Abuse prevention and aggregate statistics rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and free of spam.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in provider, which stores the data above.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and, like any web host, sees your IP address when you load it.
  • OpenStreetMap: serves the map images, so it sees your IP address and which area of the map you're viewing.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Our email provider: sends sign-in emails to your address.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

Your device's storage

We don't use tracking cookies. The app saves a few things in your browser's local storage: your settings (theme, prayer-time method), the private keys for beacons you host or join, and your sign-in session if you have an account. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, for it to be corrected, or for it to be deleted. You can delete your account yourself in the app (account icon → Delete account), which removes your email, name and every link between you and any beacon. For anything else, email amaan78940@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
